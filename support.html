Practical Adaptations

AdaptationWho it supports
Use short, simple instructions and numbered steps.Learners who struggle to process long or complex instructions.
Use large, readable labels and uncluttered graphs.Learners who experience visual or reading difficulties.
Add captions to teacher-created videos.Learners with hearing difficulties and learners who benefit from reading while listening.
Provide downloadable/printable learning material.Learners with limited device or internet access and learners needing extra practice.

Accessibility Reminder

Images used on the website should include meaningful alternative text (alt text). Graphs should have clear titles, labels and scales.

Every learner should have a fair opportunity to participate and learn.
