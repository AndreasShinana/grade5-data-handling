Interactive Learning Activity

Our Favourite Fruit Survey

HomeLearnActivityAssessmentLearner SupportDigital CitizenshipReflection

Instructions

  1. Work in a group of 4–5 learners.
  2. Choose five fruits, such as apple, banana, orange, mango and watermelon.
  3. Ask classmates which fruit they prefer. Do not record names or personal information.
  4. Use tally marks to record the answers.
  5. Convert the tallies into a frequency table.
  6. Choose a suitable scale and create a vertical bar graph using a spreadsheet or digital graphing tool.
  7. Give your graph a clear title and label the axes.
  8. Answer the questions below and present your findings.

Investigation Questions

  1. Which fruit was the most popular?
  2. Which fruit was the least popular?
  3. How many learners were included in the survey?
  4. What conclusion can you make from your data?
  5. If the survey were repeated with another class, what might you predict?

ICT Connection

Use a digital spreadsheet or graphing tool to enter your frequency data and produce a bar graph. Learners work collaboratively, discuss the results and explain their graph to the class.

Teacher Facilitation

The teacher guides learners with questions, checks their scales and labels, and encourages learners to explain how their graph represents the collected data. This supports a learner-centred approach because learners actively collect, organise, represent and interpret their own data.

Keep learner information private when collecting classroom data.
