Responsible Online Participation

  • Do not publish learners' names, personal information or photographs.
  • Respect the ideas and opinions of other learners.
  • Use reliable educational sources.
  • Acknowledge images, videos and other resources created by others.
  • Do not copy another person's work and present it as your own.
  • Use digital devices for safe and appropriate learning activities.

Copyright

When using an image, video or other resource from another creator, acknowledge the source. Prefer teacher-created resources or resources that permit educational use.

Privacy

For the class survey, collect only the information needed for the mathematics activity. Do not publish learner names, contact details, photographs or other personal information.

Think before you share.
