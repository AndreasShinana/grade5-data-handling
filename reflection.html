Reflection

I selected Data Handling because it is a practical Grade 5 Mathematics topic that can easily be connected to learners’ everyday experiences. Learners can collect information about familiar topics, organise the information and represent it using tables, pictograms and bar graphs.

The teaching approach used is learner-centred. Instead of only listening to an explanation, learners participate in a survey, record their own data, create a graph and explain what the graph shows. The teacher acts as a facilitator by giving guidance, asking questions and checking learners’ understanding.

ICT is integrated through a digital learning website, digital graphing or spreadsheet tools and an online assessment. The website gives learners access to explanations, examples and activities in one place. The digital assessment also gives learners an opportunity to apply their knowledge after the practical activity.

One possible challenge is that not all learners may have equal access to devices or may have the same level of digital experience. To address this, learners can work in pairs or groups and printable materials can be provided. The website also uses short instructions, readable text, clear graphs and captions where videos are used.

Digital citizenship is included because learners need to understand that classroom data should be handled responsibly. Learners should not publish personal information or photographs, and resources from other creators should be acknowledged.

If I improved the website in future, I would add more interactive graphing tasks, short teacher-created videos and extension activities for learners who finish early. I would also include more practice questions so learners can strengthen their ability to interpret different data sets.
